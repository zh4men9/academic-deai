# Validation Rubric

Use this rubric in final audit mode after any rewrite.

## 1. Meaning preserved

- Pass: the revised text keeps the original technical and argumentative meaning
- Borderline: the meaning is mostly preserved but one phrasing choice may narrow or broaden scope
- Fail: the rewrite changes what the text actually claims

## 2. Claim strength preserved or properly softened

- Pass: claims match the original evidence or become more proportionate under the chosen claim mode
- Borderline: one sentence sounds slightly stronger or weaker than intended
- Fail: novelty, significance, or generality was materially altered

## 3. Citation and evidence relationships preserved

- Pass: attribution and evidence anchors remain intact
- Borderline: one sentence could blur what evidence supports which claim
- Fail: the rewrite changes attribution, scope, or evidential linkage

## 4. Detail preserved

- Pass: named algorithms, datasets, environments, enumerations, and scoped qualifiers that matter remain intact
- Borderline: one concrete detail was compressed in a way that is still recoverable from context
- Fail: meaningful detail was dropped or obscured

## 5. Academic tone naturalized without becoming informal

- Pass: the text sounds less templated while staying formal and scholarly
- Borderline: the text is cleaner but still somewhat mechanical, or slightly too smooth
- Fail: the text becomes casual, promotional, chatty, or overly conversational

## 6. Sentence rhythm improved without introducing generic prose

- Pass: the prose reads more naturally and more specifically
- Borderline: rhythm improves but one edit becomes more generic
- Fail: the rewrite sounds smoother at the cost of specificity or discipline fit

## 7. Manual Check coverage

- Pass: every changed high-risk or citation-sensitive edit that needs review appears once in `Manual Check Items`, and the checklist is useful rather than noisy
- Borderline: the checklist is mildly noisy or misses a low-severity item
- Fail: the checklist misses a material changed review item or floods the reviewer with low-value items

## 8. Transparent reporting coverage

- Pass: `Manual Check Items`, `Skipped High-Risk Items`, and `Unchanged Suspicious Items` are separated correctly
- Borderline: one residual item is reported in the wrong bucket but still visible
- Fail: residual uncertainty is hidden, flattened, or misreported

## 9. Claim mode consistency

- Pass: the selected claim mode is applied consistently across similar sentences
- Borderline: one sentence drifts slightly from the selected mode
- Fail: the output mixes incompatible calibration styles without explanation

## Audit Decision

- Safe: all nine categories pass, or only minor borderline cases remain
- Needs revision: one or more important borderline cases remain
- Unsafe: any fail is present
