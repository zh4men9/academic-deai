# Validation Rubric

Use this rubric in final audit mode after any rewrite.

## 1. Meaning preserved

- Pass: the revised text keeps the original technical and argumentative meaning
- Borderline: the meaning is mostly preserved but one phrasing choice may narrow or broaden scope
- Fail: the rewrite changes what the text actually claims

## 2. Claim strength preserved or properly softened

- Pass: claims match the original evidence or become more proportionate
- Borderline: one sentence sounds slightly stronger or weaker than intended
- Fail: novelty, significance, or generality was materially altered

## 3. Citation and evidence relationships preserved

- Pass: attribution and evidence anchors remain intact
- Borderline: one sentence could blur what evidence supports which claim
- Fail: the rewrite changes attribution, scope, or evidential linkage

## 4. Academic tone naturalized without becoming informal

- Pass: the text sounds less templated while staying formal and scholarly
- Borderline: the text is cleaner but still somewhat mechanical, or slightly too smooth
- Fail: the text becomes casual, promotional, chatty, or overly conversational

## 5. Sentence rhythm improved without introducing generic prose

- Pass: the prose reads more naturally and more specifically
- Borderline: rhythm improves but one edit becomes more generic
- Fail: the rewrite sounds smoother at the cost of specificity or discipline fit

## 6. Manual Check coverage

- Pass: every high-risk or citation-sensitive edit that needs review appears once in `Manual Check Items`, and the checklist is useful rather than noisy
- Borderline: the checklist is mildly noisy or misses a low-severity item
- Fail: the checklist misses a material review item or floods the reviewer with low-value items

## Audit Decision

- Safe: all six categories pass, or only minor borderline cases remain
- Needs revision: one or more important borderline cases remain
- Unsafe: any fail is present
